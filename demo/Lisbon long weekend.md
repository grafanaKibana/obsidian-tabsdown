# Lisbon · September 17–23

Long weekend, no car. One fixed thing each day and enough empty time to change our minds.

````tabsdown
```tabsdown
config: position=left

tab: Thu 17 (Day 0)
### Arrive and stay nearby

Land in the afternoon. Drop the bags, buy water and breakfast things, then walk until dinner rather than crossing the city immediately.

- Metro from the airport unless the flight is badly delayed
- Find the nearest grocery shop
- Dinner somewhere around [[Arroios]]

Do not schedule a sunset viewpoint. If we still have energy, we will find one.

tab: Fri 18 (Day 1)
### Alfama and the river

Start early before the steep streets get busy.

- [[Sé Cathedral]] and the lanes behind it
- Coffee when we find a place with an empty table
- Walk down toward the river
- Meet Rui after work

Possible detour: ferry from Cais do Sodré if the weather is clear and nobody wants another museum.

tab: Sat 19 (Day 2)
### Belém, slowly

The only real plan is the west side of the city.

- Tram or train to [[Belém]]
- [[Jerónimos Monastery]] from outside if the queue is long
- River walk
- Late lunch

Keep the afternoon open. [[LX Factory]] is a fallback, not a requirement.

tab: Sun 20 (Day 3)
### Sintra, with one plan

Take the train to [[Sintra]] early enough to avoid rushing, but choose only one major sight before lunch.

- [[Quinta da Regaleira]] if the weather is dry
- Lunch away from the busiest square
- Return when the group starts getting tired

Do not add Cascais on the same day. Keep it for another trip.

tab: Mon 21 (Day 4)
### Estrela and Campo de Ourique

A slower neighbourhood day after Sintra.

- Walk through [[Estrela Garden]]
- Lunch around [[Campo de Ourique]]
- Read or write postcards in the afternoon

Laundry tonight if the apartment has a machine.

tab: Tue 22 (Day 5)
### Across the river

Take the ferry and spend the morning walking without a checklist.

- Coffee near the terminal
- River view back toward Lisbon
- Return for a late lunch

Use the rest of the day for the place we skipped earlier in the week.

tab: Wed 23 (Day 6)
### Last morning

Check out, leave the bags, and return to whichever neighbourhood we kept talking about all weekend.

- Buy something small for Mum
- Write down the names of places we would actually revisit
- Airport with more time than seems necessary
````
